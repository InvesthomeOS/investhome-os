Look & feel

Premium light real-estate OS

Spacious, image-led commercial surfaces. Teal-slate accent on soft gray canvas. Instrument Sans + Source Serif 4 in review artifacts preview the voice: calm, confident, sales-friendly — never purple AI glow or dark trading terminal.

Reusable UI patterns

Today strip

Personal KPIs that drill to queues (follow-ups, tasks, meetings). Not a second BI product.

Match panel

Shared visual unit mini-cards from Sales drawer, Inventory card, or Customer Matches tab.

One-status card

Inventory/project faces show a single primary status; secondary statuses in drawer / Advanced.

Details ▸ / Advanced ▾

Progressive disclosure contract for fields and deep tools. Backend routes may exist; chrome stays quiet.

Identity header

Avatar · name · role badges · owner · lifecycle · channel actions before tabs.

Honest empty

Every widget declares definition, time window, source, drill, and empty CTA.

Mobile recommendations

  • Bottom tabs: Home / Sales / Inventory / More (Marketing under More or role-aware)
  • Queues and cards first; charts compress to chips or horizontal stage strips
  • Drawers become full-screen sheets; meta rails become bottom sheets
  • Never recreate desktop widget walls on phone

Accessibility & content

  • Status never by color alone — chip text + icon
  • Focus rings on primary actions; sufficient contrast on soft fills
  • i18n-ready labels; avoid jargon in default chrome
  • Loading and empty treated as first-class states
