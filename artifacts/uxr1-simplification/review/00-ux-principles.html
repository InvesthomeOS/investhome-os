Section 00 · UX Principles

Design as a real production product

Every screen in this package must help a salesperson, marketer, investor manager, or executive finish today’s work faster — not look like a generic admin template or a decorative dashboard.

Applies to Sections 03–11 Rule Backend capable · UI hides complexity
North star Role outcomes Hard rules Progressive disclosure Anti-patterns

North star

Operational over decorative

Finish today’s work

Prefer workflows (call, match, hold, ship, approve) over data density. Charts exist only when they answer a commercial question and drill somewhere useful. Empty states are honest. Advanced power stays expandable.

Who must finish faster

Salesperson

  • Clear follow-ups and stage moves without leaving the deal context
  • Match and soft-hold units visually
  • Reach the person on Call / WhatsApp / Email in one surface

Marketer

  • See production and campaigns without a 30-item rail
  • Ship blog / social / email from Content Studio
  • Open Advanced tools only when the job needs them

Investor manager

  • See capital-path people on the same profile as buyers
  • Surface holds and proposals that need attention
  • Keep KYC / ticket fields behind Details until relevant

Executive

  • Glance funnel, availability, and project health in seconds
  • No BI explorer or platform widgets on the default path
  • Trust empty states over fake charts

Hard product rules

RuleMeaning
Real product, not admin chromeImage-led inventory/projects; sales-native stages; marketing as a studio.
One job per surfaceDashboard routes work; Sales moves deals; Inventory sells stock; Marketing ships.
Workflow > densityPrimary actions and queues beat multi-widget decoration.
Progressive disclosureDaily fields visible; Advanced / Details expand for power and compliance.
Preserve backendsHide/archive in navigation only after approval — do not delete modules or APIs in UXR1 design.
Chart honestyAllowed exceptions: sales funnel + unit availability donut. Everything else must earn its place.

Progressive disclosure pattern

Default (daily)

  • Identity, status, next action, money band, unit/project link
  • Primary CTAs that finish a task
  • One status language on cards

Hidden until needed

  • UTM / attribution internals · score breakdowns
  • Finance P&L · permits · legal enums
  • Platform, automation, AI telemetry, BI explorer
  • Marketing Advanced channels and vendors

Anti-patterns (reject in review)

  • Decorative dashboards, placeholder charts, or “widget walls” with no drill
  • Generic SaaS admin tables as the default for units or projects
  • Dual CRM + Marketing sidebars competing for the same person
  • Exposing G15 / platform / design-system spikes next to daily work
  • Purple AI glow aesthetics or dark trading-terminal skins
